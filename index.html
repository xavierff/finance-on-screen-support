FINANCE ON SCREEN

Support

Finance on Screen helps you learn finance naturally through rotating Lock Screen widgets and a simple finance library.

Add the Lock Screen widget

Press and hold your iPhone Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen, then tap the widget area below the clock. Find Finance on Screen and add the rectangular widget.

Widget not appearing?

Open Finance on Screen at least once after installation, then try adding the widget again.

Privacy

Finance on Screen is designed to work primarily offline.
